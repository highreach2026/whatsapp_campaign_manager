# High Reach WhatsApp lead-generation copy

## Recommended approved marketing template

**Template name**

`high_reach_services_intro`

**Category**

Marketing

**Header**

Image

Use one strong High Reach service/brand visual. Avoid stuffing the visual with tiny text.

**Body**

Hi {{1}}, looking for the right creative and growth partner for {{2}}?

High Reach brings Branding, Marketing, Content, Web, Technology and AI together to help businesses reach higher.

Reply **INTERESTED** and we’ll understand what your business needs and suggest the right next step.

Reply **STOP** anytime to opt out.

**Suggested buttons**

- Quick reply: `INTERESTED`
- Website: `Visit High Reach` -> `https://highreach.media`

Keep the exact final button configuration aligned with what WhatsApp Manager allows for the template type in your account.

# Opt-in copy for the High Reach website / lead form

Place an unticked checkbox beside the lead form:

> I agree to receive WhatsApp messages from High Reach about my enquiry, relevant services, offers and updates. I can opt out anytime by replying STOP.

Recommended data to record with the lead:

- phone number
- consent checkbox = true
- date/time of consent
- consent source (e.g. `website_contact_form`)
- page/form used
- name/company when supplied

Do not pre-tick the consent checkbox.

# Lead funnel

Recommended campaign path:

1. Website form / Meta lead ad / click-to-WhatsApp / QR code captures a genuinely interested lead.
2. Store the contact with proof/source of WhatsApp consent.
3. Send the approved High Reach marketing template.
4. Lead taps or replies `INTERESTED`.
5. A High Reach team member qualifies the requirement.
6. Move the lead into the appropriate service pipeline.
7. Stop promotional messaging immediately if the lead opts out.

# Recommended segmentation

Avoid one generic blast to everyone. Build smaller segments such as:

- Branding & Creative
- Digital Marketing / Performance
- Web & App
- AI & Automation
- Content / Film / Production
- E-commerce
- Existing clients
- Warm enquiries that did not close

Create a separate approved marketing template for materially different offers instead of forcing every service into one message.
